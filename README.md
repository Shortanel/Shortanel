# 🐦‍⬛ Knowing the Crow

Hello there, I'm **Valon Shortanel Corvius III** or **Shorty**, your friendly interdimensional crow.

As every bird of my species, I tend to be a bit of a kleptomaniac — but not for banal things like a piece of jewelry. Oh no, what your crow here searches for across many universes are good **stories**. Stories that can make you cry, laugh, scream in terror — but most importantly, they become a part of you.

With the passage of eons, I've come to think it's time to share some of these stories with you, through a medium that can make them most impactful: **GAMES**! — so that you can create new ones through them yourself.

So enjoy, and be grateful — the mighty Shorty lets you experience stories and worlds you never knew existed.

# 👾 Playing by your games

Across my travels I've watched you humans invent countless ways to tell your stories — drawing, poetry, music, the written word, moving pictures. Clever, all of them. Each makes a story flavor unique in their way.

But none of them let a story crawl inside a person and stay there quite like what you humans call a game.

So that is the tongue I chose to learn — not just to tell my stories well, which they already are, but to make them yours. To let you live inside them, rather than let them pass you by like just another fleeting moment of your life.
